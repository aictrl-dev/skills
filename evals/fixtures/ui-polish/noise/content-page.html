Writing things down

A short essay on keeping team knowledge.

Why it matters

Most of what a team knows lives in people's heads. When someone is away, the answer is away too. Writing it down is the first step to sharing it.

Notes do not need to be perfect. A rough page that exists beats a polished page that does not.

The best time to write it down is right after you explained it twice.

Start small

Pick one question you answer every week and write the answer once. Link to it next time.

Then pick the next one. After a month the team has a small library that answers the common questions.

Keep it current

Put a date on each page and a name next to it. When something changes, the person who changed it updates the page.

Old pages are fine as long as they say they are old.

Review the list once a quarter and archive what nobody opens.
